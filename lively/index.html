What this demo shows

  • 12 filter looks with an intensity blend, plus exposure, contrast, saturation and more — applied to the playing clip in real time.
  • A soundtrack lane: add music or record a voiceover, trim it on the waveform, slide it along the timeline, set volume and fades, and mix it with the original audio.
  • Trim with a filmstrip, and a baked video export.

What needs the iPhone

Real Live Photos are an iOS format — a paired photo and video with matching identifiers. The Lively app edits the ones in your library and saves genuine Live Photos back (and converts videos into them). This page brings the same editor feel to the browser with standard web video.

Live Photo editor · runs on your device

Give your clips
a life of their own.

Grade a Live Photo or any short video with 16 looks and 14 fine adjustments, turn it into a Loop, a Bounce or a Long Exposure, lay a soundtrack under it, and export MP4, GIF or the perfect still.

or drop a .MOV / .MP4 anywhere on this page

Nothing is uploaded — decoding, grading and export all happen in this tab.

Or start with a sample

  • Looks & light16 looks with an amount dial, plus exposure, highlights, shadows, warmth, tint, grain and more — graded on the GPU while it plays.
  • Live effectsLive, Loop with a cross-faded seam, Bounce, and a real Long Exposure blend — the four iPhone effects.
  • SoundAdd music, record a voiceover or pick a built-in track; slide and trim it on the timeline with fades.
  • ExportFrame-accurate MP4 with sound, a looping GIF, or the key photo as a full-resolution JPEG.
